Professional Biography

Pravin Thapar stands out as a beacon of advocacy and social justice within the legal community. After a distinguished career as a chartered accountant and financial advisor, Pravin was drawn to the law out of a deep-seated desire to address systemic inequities and to ensure that justice serves all members of society, not just the privileged few. His transition to law was motivated by his exposure to the financial hardships and legal misunderstandings faced by many underrepresented groups. Today, he is not only a trusted advocate in civil, corporate, and family law but also a passionate defender of human rights through his legal practice.

Pravin is the founder of the CHALAU Council of Hope and Legal Aid, an organization dedicated to championing the rights of the underprivileged. Through CHALAU, he has mobilized a network of legal professionals committed to pro bono work, fostering a culture of service within the legal community. His work extends beyond the courtroom to include public speaking engagements, workshops, and seminars aimed at educating the public on legal rights and responsibilities.

Legal Philosophy and Approach

Pravin's legal philosophy is rooted in a commitment to integrity, transparency, and empathy. He believes that effective legal representation goes beyond mere advocacy to encompass a holistic understanding of each client's circumstances. This approach has been particularly effective in his practice areas, enabling him to offer nuanced advice in complex financial and family law cases. His expertise in financial matters brings a unique perspective to his legal practice, particularly in corporate law and cases involving financial disputes.
